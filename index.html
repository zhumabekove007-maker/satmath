Traps almost everyone falls into

These are the specific mistakes that cost the most points. Each one has a short question so you can see the trap happen.

Reading and setup

1

Answering for the wrong quantity

Setup

The question asks for 6x + 2, or y, or x − 2, and you hand back x. Underline what is being asked before you touch the algebra.

If 3x + 1 = 13, what is the value of 6x + 2?

  1. A4
  2. B13
  3. C26
  4. D27

Show answer
C (26). Solving gives x = 4, which is answer A, the trap. But 6x + 2 = 2(3x + 1) = 2 · 13 = 26, with no solving needed.
2

Unit conversions

Setup

Area and volume conversions square and cube the length factor. 1 yd² = 9 ft², not 3 ft².

A floor is 12 ft by 15 ft. Carpet is sold by the square yard. How many square yards are needed?

Show answer
20. Area = 180 ft², and 180 ÷ 9 = 20. Dividing by 3 gives 60, the trap.
3

Must be vs. could be

Setup

"Must be true" needs to hold for every allowed value. One counterexample kills a choice.

n is a positive multiple of 6. Which of the following must be true?

  1. An is a multiple of 4
  2. Bn is a multiple of 3
  3. Cn is a multiple of 9
  4. Dn is a multiple of 12

Show answer
B. n = 6 is a multiple of 3 but not of 4, 9, or 12. Those choices could be true but are not guaranteed.
4

Ignoring the real-world context

Setup

Check whether the answer has to be a whole number, positive, or under a limit. The math result may not be the answer.

Tickets cost $8 each. What is the maximum number of tickets that can be bought with $100?

Show answer
12. 100 ÷ 8 = 12.5, and you cannot buy half a ticket. Rounding up to 13 would cost $104.

Algebra

5

Distributing a negative sign

Algebra

A minus in front of parentheses flips every term inside. Write the parentheses out; do not do it in your head.

Which expression is equivalent to 7 − (3x − 2)?

  1. A5 − 3x
  2. B9 − 3x
  3. C5 + 3x
  4. D9 + 3x

Show answer
B. 7 − 3x + 2 = 9 − 3x. Forgetting to flip the −2 gives A.
6

No solution or infinitely many

Algebra

Do not solve. Compare coefficients. Same slope with a different constant means no solution. Everything matching means infinitely many.

3(x + 2) = 3x + k has infinitely many solutions. What is k?

Show answer
6. The left side expands to 3x + 6. For the two sides to be identical, k = 6. If k were any other value, there would be no solution.
7

Flipping the inequality

Algebra

Multiplying or dividing by a negative reverses the sign. Test one value from your answer at the end.

Which is the solution to −2x + 5 > 11?

  1. Ax > −3
  2. Bx < −3
  3. Cx > 3
  4. Dx < 3

Show answer
B. −2x > 6 and dividing by −2 flips the sign: x < −3. Check x = −4: 8 + 5 = 13 > 11 ✓.
8

Extraneous solutions

Algebra

Squaring both sides can create answers that do not work. Plug every result back into the original equation.

What is the solution to √(x + 6) = x?

Show answer
3. Squaring gives x² − x − 6 = 0, so x = 3 or x = −2. Check x = −2: √4 = 2, not −2, so it is extraneous.
9

Absolute value has two cases

Algebra

|expression| = c splits into expression = c and expression = −c.

What is the sum of the solutions to |2x − 3| = 7?

Show answer
3. 2x − 3 = 7 gives x = 5, and 2x − 3 = −7 gives x = −2. Sum: 5 + (−2) = 3.
10

Squaring a binomial

Algebra

(a + b)² is a² + 2ab + b². The middle term is not optional.

Which is equivalent to (x + 3)²?

  1. Ax² + 9
  2. Bx² + 6x + 9
  3. Cx² + 3x + 9
  4. D2x + 6

Show answer
B. Test x = 1: (1 + 3)² = 16, and B gives 1 + 6 + 9 = 16. Choice A gives 10.

Percents and exponentials

11

Successive percent changes

Percents

Percent changes multiply; they do not add. Use factors: +20% is × 1.2, −20% is × 0.8.

A $100 item goes up 20%, then the new price goes down 20%. What is the final price?

Show answer
$96. 100 × 1.2 × 0.8 = 96, a net 4% decrease, not 0%.
12

Percent vs. percentage points

Percents

A change in a percentage is measured in points. The percent change compares to the starting value.

A rate rises from 40% to 50%. What is the percent increase?

Show answer
25%. The rise is 10 percentage points, and 10 ÷ 40 = 0.25.
13

Growth factor vs. rate

Percents

Decreasing by 20% each period means multiplying by 0.8, not 0.2. Factor = 1 ± rate.

A car worth $20,000 loses 20% of its value each year. Which model gives its value V after t years?

  1. AV = 20,000(0.2)t
  2. BV = 20,000(0.8)t
  3. CV = 20,000 − 0.2t
  4. DV = 20,000(1.2)t

Show answer
B. Each year keeps 80% of the value. After 2 years: 20,000 × 0.8² = $12,800.
14

Time in the exponent

Percents

"Every k units" puts t/k in the exponent, not t.

A population of 500 doubles every 3 years. Which gives its size after t years?

  1. A500(2)t
  2. B500(2)3t
  3. C500(2)t/3
  4. D500(3)t/2

Show answer
C. At t = 9: 500 × 2³ = 4,000. Choice A would give 500 × 512.

Quadratics and functions

15

Pick the form the question needs

Functions

Factored form shows zeros, vertex form shows the vertex, standard form shows the y-intercept. Do not convert if the form already answers the question.

What is the minimum value of y = (x − 2)(x − 6)?

Show answer
−4. The zeros are 2 and 6, so the vertex is at their midpoint, x = 4. Then y = (4 − 2)(4 − 6) = −4.
16

Direction of horizontal shifts

Functions

f(x + 3) moves left 3, not right. Inside changes act opposite to how they look.

The graph of f has its vertex at (2, 1). Where is the vertex of g(x) = f(x + 3)?

Show answer
(−1, 1). Shifting left by 3 moves the x-coordinate from 2 to −1.
17

Reading a circle equation

Functions

In (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r², the center is (h, k) and the right side is r², not r. Watch the sign flip.

What are the center and radius of (x − 3)² + (y + 2)² = 25?

Show answer
Center (3, −2), radius 5. y + 2 means k = −2. And 25 = r², so r = 5.
18

Discriminant and number of solutions

Functions

b² − 4ac > 0 gives two real solutions, = 0 gives one, < 0 gives none.

For which values of k does x² + 6x + k = 0 have no real solutions?

Show answer
k > 9. The discriminant is 36 − 4k, and 36 − 4k < 0 gives k > 9.

Geometry and trigonometry

19

Scaling area and volume

Geometry

If lengths scale by k, area scales by k² and volume by k³.

A cone has volume 10 cm³. A similar cone has every dimension tripled. What is its volume?

Show answer
270 cm³. 10 × 3³ = 270. Multiplying by 3 gives 30, the trap.
20

Radians vs. degrees

Geometry

Check the Desmos angle mode (wrench icon) before every trig calculation. The wrong mode gives a confident wrong answer.

You type sin(30) in Desmos and get −0.988. What happened?

Show answer
Radian mode. In degree mode, sin(30) = 0.5. Switch the mode in the wrench menu.
21

sin x = cos(90° − x)

Geometry

Sine and cosine of complementary angles are equal. Look for this whenever both functions appear.

If sin(x°) = 4/5, what is cos(90° − x°)?

Show answer
4/5. cos(90° − x°) equals sin(x°) by the complementary-angle relationship.
22

Matching similar triangles

Geometry

Match corresponding sides by vertex order. In △ABC ~ △DEF, AB pairs with DE, BC with EF.

△ABC ~ △DEF. AB = 6, DE = 9, and BC = 8. What is EF?

Show answer
12. The scale factor is 9/6 = 1.5, and 8 × 1.5 = 12.

Data and statistics

23

Conditional probability denominators

Data

"Given that" names the group you are inside. That row or column total is your denominator, not the grand total.

Of 100 students, 40 are juniors. 25 of the juniors play a sport. If a junior is chosen at random, what is the probability the student plays a sport?

Show answer
25/40 = 5/8. The denominator is the 40 juniors, not the 100 students.
24

Causation and generalization

Data

Random sampling lets you generalize to that population. Random assignment lets you claim cause and effect. Check for each separately.

500 members were randomly chosen from a city's gyms. Sauna users slept longer on average. What conclusion is supported?

Show answer
Sauna users among that city's gym members tend to sleep longer. Members were sampled randomly, so generalizing to them is fine. Nobody was randomly assigned to use the sauna, so you cannot say it causes longer sleep.
25

Margin of error

Data

A margin of error gives a plausible range, not a guarantee.

A poll reports 52% support with a margin of error of ±3%. What is the plausible range for true support?

Show answer
49% to 55%. It is the most plausible range, but the true value is not guaranteed to lie inside it.
26

Outliers and the median

Data

Extreme values pull the mean and the standard deviation a lot. The median barely moves.

For 4, 5, 6, 7, 8 the mean and median are both 6. What happens if 8 is changed to 80?

Show answer
The mean becomes 20.4; the median stays 6. Only the middle value counts for the median.
27

Weighted averages

Data

Combine groups by their sizes. Never average two group means directly.

Class A has 10 students with a mean of 90. Class B has 30 students with a mean of 70. What is the combined mean?

Show answer
75. (10·90 + 30·70) ÷ 40 = 3,000 ÷ 40 = 75. Averaging 90 and 70 gives 80, the trap.
28

Interpreting a line of best fit

Data

Slope means change in y per one unit of x. Intercept means predicted y at x = 0. Say both with units.

A model is score = 2.5h + 40, where h is hours studied. Interpret 2.5 and predict the score for h = 4.

Show answer
2.5 is the predicted score increase per additional hour studied. At h = 4, the predicted score is 2.5(4) + 40 = 50.

Student-produced responses

29

Rounding

Grid-in

If the question does not ask you to round, give the exact value. A fraction avoids rounding errors entirely.

The answer works out to 0.666666… How should you enter it?

Show answer
2/3. The exact fraction is accepted. Entering 0.66 would be marked wrong.
30

No units or percent signs

Grid-in

The answer box takes numbers only. Enter 25, not 25% or 25 ft.

You find that the answer is "25 percent." What do you type?

Show answer
25. The question defines the unit, so only the number goes in the box.
31

Negative signs

Grid-in

Read your answer once more for the sign before you submit.

x + 9 = 4. Which entry is correct?

  1. A5
  2. B−5
  3. C−13
  4. D13

Show answer
B (−5). The check is quick: −5 + 9 = 4.

Use Desmos for everything it can do

The built-in calculator is the biggest time-saver on the test. These are the moves worth practicing until they are automatic.

Graphing shortcuts

32

Solve a system by graphing

Desmos

Type both equations. Tap the gray dot where the lines cross to read the solution.

Solve y = 2x + 1 and y = −x + 7.

Show answer
(2, 5). Type both lines and tap the intersection. Check: 2(2) + 1 = 5 and −2 + 7 = 5 ✓.
33

Solve an equation with two graphs

Desmos

Graph the left side and the right side as two separate curves. Each x-value where they cross is a solution.

Solve x² − 3 = 2x.

Show answer
x = −1 and x = 3. Enter y = x² − 3 and y = 2x, then read the x-coordinates of the two intersections.
34

Zeros and vertices in one click

Desmos

Type the function and tap the gray dots. You get zeros, vertex, and y-intercept without any algebra.

What is the vertex of y = x² − 4x − 5?

Show answer
(2, −9). Graph it and tap the minimum. The zeros show up as −1 and 5 as well.
35

Equivalent-expression questions

Desmos

Type the original expression, then each choice. The choice whose graph lies exactly on top of it is equivalent. Or type original − choice and look for a flat line at 0.

Which is equivalent to (x + 2)² − (x − 1)²?

  1. A6x + 3
  2. B6x + 5
  3. C2x + 3
  4. Dx² + 3

Show answer
A. The original graphs as a straight line, y = 6x + 3. Only choice A overlaps it.
36

Test points in inequality systems

Desmos

Graph the inequalities, then type each answer choice as a point. The one inside the overlap is the solution.

Which point satisfies y > x + 1 and y < −x + 5?

  1. A(1, 3)
  2. B(3, 5)
  3. C(0, 0)
  4. D(4, 1)

Show answer
A. Type (1, 3): 3 > 2 ✓ and 3 < 4 ✓. Point (3, 5) fails the second inequality since 5 < 2 is false.

Sliders, regression, and statistics

37

Use sliders for "for what value of k"

Desmos

Type the equation with k in it and click the slider prompt. Drag k until the condition happens.

For what value of k does the system y = 2x + 3 and y = kx + 7 have no solution?

Show answer
2. Drag k until the lines become parallel. That happens at k = 2, since the slopes match but the intercepts differ.
38

Regression from a table

Desmos

Enter the data in a table. Type y₁ ~ mx₁ + b for a line or y₁ ~ a·b^x₁ for an exponential, and read the fitted values.

Points (1, 3), (2, 5), (3, 7). Find the line through them.

Show answer
y = 2x + 1. Desmos shows m = 2 and b = 1. For (0, 5), (1, 10), (2, 20) the exponential fit gives a = 5, b = 2.
39

Mean, median, and standard deviation

Desmos

Use mean([…]), median([…]), and stdev([…]). Do not compute them by hand.

Find the mean and median of 4, 8, 15, 16, 23, 42.

Show answer
Mean 18, median 15.5. Type mean([4,8,15,16,23,42]) and median([4,8,15,16,23,42]).
40

Type long arithmetic straight in

Desmos

Compound-interest and exponent arithmetic goes right into an expression line.

What is 3000(1.04)⁵ to the nearest cent?

Show answer
$3,649.96. Type 3000(1.04)^5 in one line.
41

Restrict a domain

Desmos

Add {t ≥ 0} after an equation to draw only the meaningful part of a model.

A ball's height is h = −16t² + 64t. Find its maximum height.

Show answer
64 ft. Graph it with {t ≥ 0} and tap the peak at t = 2.
42

Graph any circle, even unsimplified

Desmos

You do not have to complete the square. Type the equation as given and read the center off the graph.

Find the center and radius of x² + y² − 6x + 4y = 12.

Show answer
Center (3, −2), radius 5. Desmos draws it directly. By hand: (x − 3)² + (y + 2)² = 12 + 13 = 25.

Speed moves that need no calculator

Some questions are faster without opening Desmos. These work on paper or in your head.

Answer-choice strategies

43

Backsolve

Strategy

With numeric choices, plug in B or C first. Too big or too small tells you which way to go.

What is the positive solution to 2x² − 3x = 20?

  1. A3
  2. B4
  3. C5
  4. D6

Show answer
B (4). Try C: 2(25) − 15 = 35, too big, so go lower. Try B: 32 − 12 = 20 ✓.
44

Pick numbers

Strategy

For variable-heavy or percent questions, choose an easy value (c = 100) and test each choice.

A store marks up cost c by 40%, then sells at 25% off the marked price. What is the selling price?

  1. A1.05c
  2. B1.15c
  3. C1.40c
  4. D0.65c

Show answer
A. Let c = 100. Marked up: 140. Then 25% off: 140 × 0.75 = 105 = 1.05c.
45

Read the choices first

Strategy

The choices show the form the answer must take. That tells you what to solve for.

Which equation represents the line through (0, 4) and (2, 0)?

  1. Ay = −2x + 4
  2. By = 2x + 4
  3. Cy = −½x + 4
  4. Dy = ½x + 4

Show answer
A. Every choice has intercept 4, so only the slope matters: (0 − 4)/(2 − 0) = −2.
46

Estimate and eliminate

Strategy

Round to easy numbers to cut choices before you calculate.

About what is 19.8% of 4,010?

  1. A79.4
  2. B794
  3. C7,940
  4. D79,400

Show answer
B. 20% of 4,000 = 800, so only 794 is close.
47

Do not simplify unless needed

Strategy

If the equation is already factored, the answers are already visible.

Solve (x − 2)(x + 5) = 0.

Show answer
x = 2 and x = −5. Expanding first would only add steps.

Formulas and facts to have memorized

48

Compare coefficients

Shortcut

For "no solution" systems, set the coefficient ratios equal, then confirm the constants do not match.

6x + ky = 12 and 3x + 4y = 5 have no solution. What is k?

Show answer
8. 6/3 = k/4, so k = 8. The constants fail the match: 12/5 ≠ 2 ✓.
49

Vertex, sum, product, and remainder

Shortcut

Vertex x = −b/2a. Roots sum to −b/a and multiply to c/a. The remainder of f(x) ÷ (x − a) is f(a).

What is the remainder when x³ − 2x + 1 is divided by x − 2?

Show answer
5. f(2) = 8 − 4 + 1 = 5.
50

Memorize squares, triples, and powers of i

Shortcut

Squares up to 20, the triples 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25, and the four-step cycle of i.

A right triangle has legs 10 and 24. What is the hypotenuse? And what is i43?

Show answer
26, and −i. 10–24–26 is 5–12–13 doubled. For i⁴³, 43 leaves remainder 3 when divided by 4, and i³ = −i.

Pacing and test-day habits

Each module has 22 questions in 35 minutes, about 1:35 per question. Questions rise in difficulty, so easy ones fund the hard ones.

Q1–8about 8 minEasy. Work fast, about 1 min each.
Q9–16about 12 minMedium. Around 1:30 each.
Q17–22about 15 minHard. Up to 2:30 each.
51

Bank time early

Pacing

Finish the first 8 questions in about 8 minutes. That leaves the hardest ones roughly 2 minutes or more each.

You finish Q1–8 in 8 minutes instead of 12. How much extra time did you bank?

Show answer
4 minutes, which is enough for two extra hard questions.
52

Halfway checkpoint

Pacing

Aim to have at least 11 questions done by 17:30. Ahead of that is fine; behind means speed up or skip.

At 17:30 you are on question 9. What should you do?

Show answer
Skip anything that stalls you and move faster on the next few. You are two questions behind.
53

Skip and flag after 90 seconds

Pacing

If you have no clear path after about 90 seconds, flag it and move on. Returning fresh often takes a fraction of the time.

You are 2 minutes into a question with no plan. What now?

Show answer
Flag it, take your best guess, and move on. Come back at the end.
54

Never leave a blank

Pacing

There is no penalty for wrong answers. Always guess after eliminating what you can.

Time is nearly up and 2 questions are blank. What do you do?

Show answer
Fill both in. A guess has a chance of being right, a blank has none.
55

Module 1 matters most

Pacing

Module 1 performance sets whether you get the harder Module 2. Be careful there, not rushed.

Which module deserves a slower, more careful pace?

Show answer
Module 1. A mistake there can lock you out of the higher-scoring Module 2.
56

Practice in Bluebook

Practice

Do full modules in the official app so the timer and Desmos feel routine.

Why practice on Bluebook rather than paper?

Show answer
You train with the real Desmos, strike-through, and timer. No surprises on test day.
57

Keep an error log

Practice

Most people repeat the same 3–4 mistakes. Log each one with the rule that would have prevented it.

You lost a point by forgetting to flip an inequality. What goes in your log?

Show answer
"Q14: divided by −2, forgot to flip. Rule: circle any negative I divide by."

Tricks for specific question types

Task-by-task moves for algebra, word problems, graphs, geometry, data, and the grid-in format.

Algebra

58

Ask for a combination? Add or subtract

Algebra

If the question wants 2x + 3y and the system is nearby, add or subtract the equations first. Do not solve for x and y separately.

If 3x + 2y = 11 and x + 4y = 9, what is 2x + 3y?

Show answer
10. Adding gives 4x + 6y = 20. Divide by 2: 2x + 3y = 10.
59

Ratios from one equation

Algebra

To find x/y, divide the given equation. Do not solve for both.

If 3x = 5y and y ≠ 0, what is x/y?

Show answer
5/3. Divide both sides by 3y.
60

Function value: solve the inside

Algebra

Given f(a) = value, set the rule equal to that value. Do not rebuild the function.

f(x) = 3x − 7 and f(a) = 11. What is a?

Show answer
6. 3a − 7 = 11, so a = 6.
61

Radical equations: backsolve

Algebra

Plugging in the choices avoids extraneous roots automatically.

Which is a solution to √(2x + 8) = x?

  1. A−2
  2. B1
  3. C4
  4. D8

Show answer
C (4). √16 = 4 ✓. Choice A fails: √4 = 2, not −2.
62

Exactly one solution

Algebra

One solution means the discriminant is 0, so b² = 4ac.

x² + bx + 16 = 0 has exactly one real solution. What is one possible value of b?

Show answer
8 (or −8). b² = 4(1)(16) = 64.

Word problems

63

Read the last sentence first

Word problem

Knowing what you are looking for makes the setup sentences easier to sort.

A tank holds 50 gal and empties 4 gal every minute. ... How many minutes until it holds 10 gal?

Show answer
10. Set 50 − 4m = 10. Reading the question first tells you to solve for m.
64

"Per" is slope, "initial" is intercept

Word problem

Words like per, each, and every point to the slope. Words like flat fee, starting, and initial point to the intercept.

A plumber charges $45 to visit plus $30 per hour. Write the cost C for h hours.

Show answer
C = 30h + 45. $30 per hour is the slope, and $45 is the starting amount.
65

Interpretation questions

Word problem

The right answer names the units and the context. Cross out any choice that swaps slope and intercept.

In C = 30h + 45, what does the 30 mean?

  1. AThe starting charge is $30
  2. BEach additional hour adds $30 to the cost
  3. CThe plumber works 30 hours
  4. DThe visit costs $75

Show answer
B. 30 is the slope, the change in cost per additional hour.
66

Model selection

Word problem

Test the first two or three table points in the choices. Two checks usually eliminate everything but one.

Table: (0, 50), (1, 60), (2, 72). Which model fits?

  1. A50 + 10x
  2. B50(1.2)x
  3. C50 + 11x
  4. D50(1.1)x

Show answer
B. At x = 1: 60 ✓. At x = 2: 50 × 1.44 = 72 ✓. Choice A gives 70 at x = 2.
67

Average speed

Word problem

Average speed is total distance divided by total time. Never average the speeds.

60 miles at 60 mph, then 60 miles at 30 mph. What is the average speed?

Show answer
40 mph. Time is 1 + 2 = 3 hours, and 120 ÷ 3 = 40. Averaging 60 and 30 gives 45, the trap.
68

Ratios into parts

Word problem

Add the ratio numbers, divide the total by that sum, then multiply.

Boys to girls is 3 : 5 in a group of 40. How many girls?

Show answer
25. 3 + 5 = 8 parts, 40 ÷ 8 = 5 per part, and 5 × 5 = 25.

Graphs and functions

69

Check the axis scale

Graphs

Gridlines are not always 1 unit apart. Read the labels on both axes before estimating.

A graph's gridlines are labeled 0, 5, 10, 15. A point sits on the third line. What is its value?

Show answer
10. The lines go 0, 5, 10, so the third line reads 10. Counting by ones would give 3.
70

Crossings equal real solutions

Graphs

The number of times a graph crosses the x-axis is the number of real solutions.

A parabola touches the x-axis at exactly one point. How many real solutions does its equation have?

Show answer
1. Touching once means one real (repeated) solution.
71

Transformations

Graphs

f(x) + k moves up, f(x + k) moves left, and −f(x) reflects over the x-axis.

If f(x) = x², where is the vertex of g(x) = −f(x) + 3, and which way does it open?

Show answer
(0, 3), opening down. The negative reflects the parabola, and +3 lifts it.
72

Table y-intercept

Graphs

If x = 0 is missing, extend the pattern backward.

x = 1, 2, 3 gives y = 7, 9, 11. What is y at x = 0?

Show answer
5. y rises by 2 per step, so one step back from 7 is 5.
73

Composition: work inside out

Graphs

With numbers given, evaluate the inner function first. No need to build the full formula.

f(x) = 2x + 1 and g(x) = x². What is f(g(3))?

Show answer
19. g(3) = 9, then f(9) = 19.
74

Match a graph fast

Graphs

Check only the y-intercept and the sign of the slope. That usually eliminates three choices.

Which graph shows y = −2x + 4?

Show answer
The line crossing the y-axis at 4 and sloping downward. Two checks are enough.

Geometry and trigonometry

75

The reference sheet is on screen

Geometry

Basic area and volume formulas are given. Spend your effort on using them quickly.

A cylinder has radius 3 and height 5. What is its volume?

Show answer
45π. V = πr²h = π · 9 · 5.
76

Circle facts

Geometry

An inscribed angle is half its central angle. Arc length is (θ/360°) · 2πr.

In a circle with radius 6, a central angle is 60°. What is the arc length?

Show answer
2π. (60/360) · 2π · 6 = (1/6) · 12π = 2π.
77

Box diagonals

Geometry

The space diagonal of a box is √(l² + w² + h²).

What is the diagonal of a 2 × 3 × 6 box?

Show answer
7. √(4 + 9 + 36) = √49 = 7.
78

Similar right triangles share trig ratios

Geometry

Read sin, cos, and tan from whichever triangle has easier numbers.

One-page cheat sheet

The reference points from this guide in one place.

Algebra

  • Negative in front of parentheses flips every term
  • Dividing by a negative flips the inequality
  • No solution: same slope, different constant
  • Infinite solutions: everything matches
  • Check radical and rational answers
  • (a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

Quadratics

  • Vertex x = −b/2a
  • Sum of roots = −b/a, product = c/a
  • Discriminant b² − 4ac: >0 two, =0 one, <0 none
  • Circle: (x − h)² + (y − k)² = r²
  • Remainder of f(x) ÷ (x − a) is f(a)

Percents and growth

  • Factor = 1 ± rate
  • Successive changes multiply
  • Percent change = change ÷ original
  • Doubles every k: 2t/k

Geometry and trig

  • Lengths × k: area × k², volume × k³
  • sin x = cos(90° − x)
  • Triples: 3-4-5, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25
  • Box diagonal √(l² + w² + h²)
  • Triangle inequality: |a − b| < c < a + b
  • Arc length (θ/360°) · 2πr

Data

  • "Given that" sets the denominator
  • Random sample: generalize. Random assignment: cause
  • Outliers move the mean, not the median
  • Combine means by weighting group sizes
  • Slope: change in y per 1 x

Grid-in and pacing

  • No units, no % sign
  • Fractions are accepted
  • Positive up to 5 characters, negative up to 6
  • Skip and flag after 90 seconds
  • Never leave a blank
